# ADR 0002 — v86 and Debian 12 i386

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-14

## Context

v86 emulates 32-bit x86 in WebAssembly; Debian 13 no longer ships an i386 kernel or installer.

## Decision

Engine `v86@0.5.471`, loaded lazily by `@lumiboot/provider-v86`, declared in `packages/catalog/src/engines.ts`. Target: Debian 12 Bookworm i386, manifests validated in `@lumiboot/vm-assets`, binaries outside Git.

## Consequences

- CLI, X11/Openbox and XFCE variants: image and profile; `running` after `LUMIBOOT_GUEST_READY` or `LUMIBOOT_DESKTOP_READY`.
- No hardware virtualization: Windows 10/11 x64 and modern macOS excluded.
